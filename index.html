This site is my digital garage, part journal, part lab, part therapy. It’s messy, inconsistent, and real. Like life. But between the ideas and the effort, I’m building something. Not to impress. Just to be honest. Just to keep going. This is me showing up, working with what I’ve got, and turning doubt into momentum.
