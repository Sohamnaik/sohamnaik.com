01 What is Tidal Genomics

Computational biology, taught like a field trip.

Tidal Genomics is a cohort-based learning brand for life sciences students in India. Each cohort takes one big idea in computational biology and walks through it together, live, in a small group, so you finish with something you did rather than something you watched.

It is built around a simple belief: you should not need a programming background to start asking questions of real biological data.

Small cohorts

Capped at 25 seats per batch, so questions get answered and nobody is lost in the crowd.

Live & guided

Daily live sessions with the instructor, and recordings shared if you miss one.

Beginner first

Made for students of biology, biotechnology, microbiology and marine science. No coding, no installs.

02 What we teach & why it helps

Reading the genomes of a whole ecosystem.

A spoonful of seawater holds thousands of microbial species, most of which cannot be grown in a lab. Metagenomics lets us sequence the DNA of the entire community at once and ask: who is there, how diverse is it, and what are they doing?

In this course you learn that way of thinking, then use it on real data, step by step, inside Galaxy, a free web platform used by researchers worldwide.

What you will learn

The logic of a metagenomic study, how sequencing data is checked and cleaned, how microbes are identified and counted, and how to compare communities.

No coding required

Every analysis runs in your browser through Galaxy. You learn what each step means and why it is there, which is the part that carries over to any tool.

Real data, real workflow

You work through the Galaxy Training Network's metagenomics tutorial using the well-known "Moving Pictures" dataset.

Something to show

You finish with a capstone report of your own analysis, and a certificate for participants who meet the completion criteria.

04 Curriculum

Seven days, one expedition.

Each day is a 75-minute live session. Days 1–5 build the skills; Days 6–7 are for putting them to work on your own capstone.

  1. 1
    Day 1

    The Invisible Ocean

    What metagenomics is, why microbial communities matter, and how a study is designed. You meet Galaxy and find your way around.

  2. 2
    Day 2

    From Sample to Sequence

    How a water or sediment sample becomes sequencing data, what the files contain, and how quality is checked before anything else.

  3. 3
    Day 3

    Who Is Out There?

    Identifying and counting the microbes in a sample, and learning to read an abundance table without getting lost.

  4. 4
    Day 4

    How Diverse Is It?

    Alpha and beta diversity, and how to compare communities across samples with plots that tell a clear story.

  5. 5
    Day 5

    From Reads to Genomes

    A guided tour of assembly and metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs): how scientists rebuild individual microbes from a mixed community.

  6. 6
    Day 6

    Capstone Studio

    A working session on your capstone analysis, with live help from the instructor as you go.

    Capstone
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Showcase & Next Steps

    Share what you found, wrap up the course, and map out where to go next in computational biology.

    Capstone

Missed a session? Every live session is recorded and shared with the cohort, so you can catch up on your own time.
